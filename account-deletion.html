Delete your CommunityHelp account

CommunityHelp users can delete their account directly in the Android app: open My profile, select Account security, then use Delete account.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email the CommunityHelp developer from the email address associated with your account. Include the words "CommunityHelp account deletion" and the account email address.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

The account login, private and public profile, saved locations, settings, notification tokens and private account notifications are deleted. Names are removed from retained request, offer and message history where needed to preserve another participant's records.

What may be retained

Safety reports and limited historic interaction records may be retained where necessary for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal obligations or the protection of other users. Retained records are no longer connected to an active public profile.

See the CommunityHelp Privacy Policy for more information.
