CommunityHelp Child Safety Standards

CommunityHelp has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Users may not use CommunityHelp to create, share, request, facilitate, promote, or distribute content involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

CommunityHelp provides in-app reporting tools for safety concerns. Reports involving suspected CSAE or CSAM may result in removal of content or accounts and reporting to relevant authorities where required by law.

CommunityHelp complies with applicable child-safety laws and cooperates with competent authorities where legally required.
